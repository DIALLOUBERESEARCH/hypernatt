## HOLD

HOLD is a first-class decision. No trade is owed to the market.
A quiet book is not a bug.

## 100% windows

A 100% win-rate window is a failure detector, not a target to
defend by filtering entries. Filtering entries to protect a
perfect window destroys more good trades than bad ones.

## What this extract is not

It is not the full private register. Closed doors, flags, and
engine constants stay private.
